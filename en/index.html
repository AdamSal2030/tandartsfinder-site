Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

Nothing. Tandartsfinder.nl is free for patients. At the practice you pay the normal, nationally fixed rates.

Am I sure the practice will accept me?

The practices on this site have indicated they have room for new patients. Places are not unlimited — the sooner you register, the sooner your turn comes.

I am in pain right now. What should I do?

For acute complaints do not wait for a registration. Call the practice directly or check its emergency page; outside opening hours they refer you to the emergency service.

Can I register my family in one go?

Yes. In the form you indicate whether you register alone, with a partner or as a family.

My city is not listed

We are adding practices all the time. Email us the name of your dentist or a practice near you — we will contact them.
