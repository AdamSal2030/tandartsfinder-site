Frequently asked questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

Nothing. Tandartsfinder.nl is free for patients. At the practice you pay the normal, nationally fixed rates.

Am I sure the practice will accept me?

The practices we work with have indicated they have room for new patients. Places are not unlimited — the sooner you register, the sooner your turn comes.

I am in pain right now. What should I do?

For acute complaints do not wait for a registration. Call your current practice if you have one, or the dental emergency service in your region.

Can I register my family in one go?

Yes. In the form you indicate whether you register alone, with a partner or as a family.

Is there a practice in my region?

We are adding practices all the time. Leave your details — as soon as a practice with room opens up near you, you will hear from us. Know a practice with room? Point them to the registration form for practices.
